## R16 Training-Data — Contested-Coverage Pilot

**Verdict: POSITIVE cheap slope (direction-only).** At fixed retained-row
count and fixed capacity, raising the audit's `legal_action_count` contested
fraction (0.20 → 0.30 → 0.45) produced a **monotone, side-balanced
Wilson-lower improvement** vs the coverage-matched baseline. The
pre-registered acceptance condition (monotone side-balanced Wilson-lower rise
as contested fraction crosses the ≥30% floor) is **met** — the
contested-coverage hypothesis is *not* falsified. Magnitude is small
(~+2.2pp side-balanced Wilson-lower across the full 0.20→0.45 span at
n=300/side, within single-gate noise); the *slope sign* is the
pre-registered signal and it is consistently positive across all three
points, on both the worst-side bound and overall WR. This unlocks (does not
itself perform) a larger-n confirmation gate; it does NOT by itself license
the expensive closed-loop / data-generation spend.

### Mechanisms as built (write scope: `training/uma_ai/dataset.py`)

Two independently-selectable knobs, both holding total retained-row count
fixed, both defaulting OFF (bit-identical no-op when unset — verified):

- **Option 3 — legal-action-count loss weighting.** `contested_loss_weight`
  (×1.0 = no-op) multiplies `_sample_weight` for rows with
  `≥ contested_min_legal` (default 4) legal actions. Reuses the existing
  `sample_weight` / `collate_policy_batch` / `normalized_weights` seam; no
  corpus change, no row-count change.
- **Option 1 — contested resampling.** `contested_resample_fraction`
  (`None` = no-op) buffers the retained stream, splits on the ≥4-legal
  threshold, and draws with replacement from each group to hit the target
  contested fraction while keeping total retained count exactly fixed
  (deterministic, RNG seeded by `--seed`). Empty-group guard yields the
  unmodified stream rather than fabricating distribution.

Threaded into `train_bc.py` (bc mode only) as `--contested-loss-weight`,
`--contested-min-legal`, `--contested-resample-fraction` — same
`--state-dim`-style option threading; no new config framework.

### Sweep (Option 1, the variable swept here)

Corpus: `runs/R7-multi-teacher-warmstart/iter-000/mixed-v3.jsonl` (the
Phase-4 v3 re-extraction of the audited R7 corpus — same 3789 retained
rows; the v2 `mixed.jsonl` cannot be loaded by the current
`cardIdsByZone`-hardened loader). Train: 8 epochs, hidden-dim 64, depth 3,
seed 7, v3.0/110-d (capacity FIXED). Gate:
`backend/src/sim/evaluateModelVsHeuristic.ts`, rule-bot, side-balanced,
300 games/side (600 total — under the n≥1000 stop-rule cap), seed-start
9000.

| Coverage point | `legal_action_count` | player WR | opp WR | **side-bal Wilson-lower** | overall WR |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| baseline (no knob) | 0.2022 | 0.290 | 0.393 | **0.2416** | 0.342 |
| resample ~30% | 0.3001 | 0.307 | 0.393 | **0.2572** | 0.350 |
| resample ~45% | 0.4500 | 0.313 | 0.397 | **0.2635** | 0.355 |

Side-balanced Wilson-lower = the worst-side Wilson-95 lower bound (the
gate's side-balanced strength signal). Monotone increase 0.2416 → 0.2572
→ 0.2635; improvement is driven by the weaker (player) side, opponent side
flat. Baseline 0.2022 reproduces the audit headline (below the ≥30% floor;
R7 retained is exactly-2-legal-dominated).

### Multi-Worker Corpus Harness (compute optimization spike)

The 50-game pilot ran 14m23s strictly single-threaded:
`evaluateModelVsHeuristic.ts` `main()` iterates games in a sequential
`for` loop, no workers. Meanwhile `evalGate.ts` already implements the
full work-stealing harness (`MCTS_WORK_STEALING_ENABLED`,
`WORK_STEALING_PREFETCH`, deterministic slot ordering) using the same
`runModelVsHeuristicGame` per-game function — but it hardcoded
`relabelMcts: false` and never serialized worker `decisionTraces`.
Closing those two gaps turns `evalGate.ts` into the multi-worker corpus
generator with no new harness.

What landed:

- `evalGate.ts` parses `--relabel-mcts` / `--relabel-state-source` and
  threads them into `EvaluateModelArgs`; the orchestrator owns the
  trace file, buffers `result.decisionTraces` by `taskIndex`, and
  flushes in ascending order at run end so the trace stream is
  byte-deterministic across `--workers` (verified bit-identical at
  N=1/16/24).
- Corpus-mode bypass: when `--relabel-mcts` is set, gate floors
  (`--min-games` / `--min-win-rate` / `--min-ci-lower`) become
  informational (still reported, no non-zero exit). A weak-model
  corpus run does not need to override floors per recipe.
- Smoke `backend/src/tests/evalGateCorpusGenSmoke.ts` locks: (1) flag
  pass-through, (2) `--workers 4` vs `--workers 1` produce identical
  sorted multisets of trace rows, (3) corpus-mode bypass exits PASS
  under absurd floors. Wired into `npm run test:train`.

Wall-clock benchmark at the pilot's exact recipe (50 games ×
modelSide=both, baseline, 50 sims, 200 rollout steps, CRN 3, rule-bot-
mirror):

| `--workers` | Wall-clock | Speedup vs N=1 |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 873.73s (14m34s) | 1.0× (matches the 14m23s pilot) |
| 16 | 149.85s (2m30s) | **5.83×** |
| 24 | 154.31s (2m34s) | 5.66× (past the parallel knee) |

Speedup below the naive 16× ceiling comes from per-game wall-time
variance (~5-30s/game) × small per-worker queue depth (~6 games each)
→ straggler tail dominates. N=24 is slightly worse, confirming the
knee. Bit-identical determinism: sorted multisets across N=1/16/24
are byte-equal (all 2088 rows). No serve_onnx contention (rule-bot-
mirror is server-free); no IPC backpressure observed on the per-task
IPC trace flush path.

Projected prod-corpus wall-clock at N=16: ~25 min total (rule-bot-
mirror ~5 min + each served recipe ~10 min, ~2× slower per recipe due
to served + 2 MCTS calls per decision for search-vs-rule). Down from
the naive ~2h sequential single-thread.

baseline numbers **bit-identically** (pair_acc 0.69815, top1
0.65123, MRR 0.79207, loss 0.69315).

Short DPO probe (3 epochs × 8093 pairs, batch 32, beta 0.1,
lr 1e-5, seed 17, hidden 64, depth 2, CUDA): **34s wall-clock**,
no NaN/divergence. Training loss decreased monotonically
(0.667 → 0.649 → 0.638). Val loss also dropped (same trajectory),
**but val accuracy regressed** (0.682 → 0.679 → 0.670) — classic
short-run overfitting.

Lift vs baseline (1620 held-out rows, deterministic split seed 17):

| Metric | Baseline | Trained | Δ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `pair_accuracy` | 0.6981 | 0.6815 | **−0.0167** |
| `ranking_accuracy_top1` | 0.6512 | 0.6191 | **−0.0321** |
| `ranking_accuracy_top2` | 0.8716 | 0.8611 | −0.0105 |
| `ranking_accuracy_top3` | 0.9321 | 0.9241 | −0.0080 |
| `mean_reciprocal_rank` | 0.7921 | 0.7731 | −0.0190 |

**Verdict: FAIL** — both pre-registered headline metrics regressed.
**The offline gate did exactly what scoping § P2 step 5 requires**:
caught the regression before any expensive closed-loop n=1000 gate
spend. No closed-loop gate runs on this trained checkpoint.

**Diagnostic from grouped metrics**:

- `action_kind`: the two largest buckets `playTrainer` (n=520)
  **−5.0%** and `attachEnergy` (n=357) **−3.4%** drove the
  regression. Smaller buckets `evolve` (+6.3%), `useAbility`
  (+6.8%), `retreatAttack` (+5.6%) improved — the policy traded
  generality for sample-rich tail wins.
- `margin_bucket`: **monotonically worse on tighter margins** —
  `>=0.20` −1.1%, `0.10-0.20` −2.2%, `0.05-0.10` **−5.8%**. The
  DPO objective is pushing larger margin flips on close pairs, but
  those flips are net wrong on the held-out.
- `source_kind`: single-source corpus (100% `mcts-relabel`, 100%
  `runner_up` negatives) — no source-mix signal to diagnose.

The implementer's recommendation lines up: longer training will
make this worse (val_acc already regressing after epoch 1). The
plausible levers are (i) HP probe (lower lr, higher beta to damp
the aggressive margin push), (ii) pair-builder diversity (top-k /
rule-bot / high-prior / policy-argmax negatives — the current
runner-up-only corpus may have a structural blind spot that maps
to the policy's failure mode).

### 3b Chunk 5b — HP Probe = POSITIVE (HP was the issue, not corpus)

Re-ran the short DPO at gentler HPs (β=0.5, lr=3e-6, 1 epoch, same
batch/seed/refs as 5a). **21s wall-clock**, val_acc=0.6879, val_loss
0.6563 (below ln 2). Eval against the same seed-17 split:

| Metric | Baseline | 5a (FAIL) | 5b (POS) | Δ vs base | Δ vs 5a |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `pair_accuracy` | 0.6981 | 0.6815 | **0.7025** | **+0.0043** | +0.0210 |
| `ranking_top1` | 0.6512 | 0.6191 | **0.6519** | **+0.0006** | +0.0327 |
| `ranking_top2` | 0.8716 | 0.8611 | 0.8728 | +0.0012 | +0.0117 |
| `ranking_top3` | 0.9321 | 0.9241 | 0.9315 | −0.0006 | +0.0074 |
| `MRR` | 0.7921 | 0.7731 | 0.7925 | +0.0004 | +0.0194 |

**Verdict: POSITIVE** — both headline metrics lift vs baseline; 5b
uniformly dominates 5a (+2-3pp per metric). HP was the issue, not
corpus diversity.

**Smoking-gun signal for HP-overfit**: `margin_bucket 0.05-0.10`
recovered from 0.5693 (5a) → 0.6350 (5b) = **+6.6pp** — the tight-
margin regression that 5a's aggressive β=0.1 + lr=1e-5 created is
reversed under gentler HPs and now exceeds baseline (+0.7pp). High-
volume `action_kind` buckets (`playTrainer` n=520 / `attachEnergy`
n=357) also recovered to ≥ baseline.

**But the lift is within noise**: at n=1620, Bernoulli SE at p=0.70
is ~0.011 (95% CI half-width ~0.022). The +0.0043 pair-accuracy lift
and +0.0006 top1 lift are well inside that band. 1 epoch likely isn't
extracting all available signal from the corpus. Next chunk: longer
training at 5b HPs to test if lift grows beyond noise. If it does →
closed-loop n=1000 gate becomes viable. If saturates/regresses →
runner-up-only corpus is the bottleneck and pair_builder mode
expansion (top-k / rule-bot / high-prior / policy-argmax) becomes
the next forward line.
